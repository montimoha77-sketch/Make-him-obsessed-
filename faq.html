Quick answers about the analysis and the book.

How to Make Him Obsessed With You is a short, practical digital guide: 17 pages, designed to be read in about 20–30 minutes.

It explains the psychology behind attraction, emotional connection, mixed signals and his behavior, so you can stop guessing what to do next.

It isn’t based on manipulation, games, or fake texting tricks.

It’s a short, personalized quiz that helps you understand the pattern behind his actions, communication and effort. You choose your situation (in a relationship, or not officially together), then answer 6 questions. It takes about 60 seconds.

Your reading includes:

Your Interest, Communication and His Effort scores
Your biggest strength
Your biggest warning

Your reading is built directly from your own answers. Each answer adds to your Interest, Communication and His Effort scores, which are shown as percentages, and the insights you see, like your biggest strength and biggest warning, are matched to where your scores land.
