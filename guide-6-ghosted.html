Why He Ghosted: 4 Mistakes Women Sometimes Make Early in Dating

First, remember this: someone ghosting you is not automatically your fault. People disappear for many reasons — avoidance, immaturity, loss of interest, or fear of confrontation.

Mistake #1: Giving More Than the Relationship Has Earned

You meet someone and immediately invest emotionally while the relationship is still new. Let consistency earn deeper access to you.

Mistake #2: Ignoring Inconsistency Because of Chemistry

Strong chemistry can make red flags look smaller than they are. Ask: "If I removed the attraction, would his behavior still look healthy?"

Mistake #3: Trying to Convince Someone to Be Interested

You can communicate clearly, but you cannot negotiate someone into wanting a relationship.

Mistake #4: Making His Silence About Your Worth

Someone else's inability or unwillingness to communicate does not define your value.

Remember: rejection is information, not a verdict on your worth. The goal of dating is to recognize the people capable of showing up, communicating, and choosing you consistently.
