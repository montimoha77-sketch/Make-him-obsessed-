Our Story

It started with a question I couldn't stop asking…

For a long time, I kept finding myself in situations with men that left me confused.

Why could a man be so interested one moment, and distant the next?

Why did some men seem to pull away the closer I got?

Why did giving more attention sometimes make things worse, while stepping back changed everything?

And honestly? I didn't always handle those situations the right way.

I overthought texts. I questioned myself.

I tried to read his mind, wondering what I had done wrong…

…and why something that felt so simple could suddenly become so complicated.

But after living these patterns again and again, I started paying attention.

I began noticing the small things so many of us overlook.

The way men communicate. The way attraction really develops.

The difference between chasing someone… and creating genuine desire.

How much our own behavior can change the dynamic between two people.

How many of us misunderstand male behavior, not because we love wrong,

…but because no one ever explained how attraction actually works.

That curiosity eventually became something much bigger.

I started putting everything I was learning into one place.

The Patterns The Lessons The Mistakes

The conversations, the breakthroughs, the things I wish I had understood much earlier.

I studied. I observed. I tested different approaches until the patterns became clear.

That's how Make Him Obsessed was born.

I created the book so women would have something real to come back to when they felt confused…

…something that helps you understand the situation instead of endlessly overthinking it.

And I built this website for the same reason: a simple place to explore these ideas and feel less lost when it comes to men and attraction.

Not born from a desire to sell something, but from a real need I lived myself.

Because I know what it feels like to sit there wondering, “What am I doing wrong?”

And I don't want you to feel like you have to figure it all out alone.

This isn't about becoming someone you're not.
