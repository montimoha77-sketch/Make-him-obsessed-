Privacy Policy

How Make Him Obsessed handles your information across the quiz, the Ask Rita chat and eBook purchases.

01

Information collected

You don't need an account or an email address to take the quiz. Depending on how you use the site, we and the tools we rely on may collect the following.

Quiz answers and results. The options you choose in the “Does He Like You?” quiz and the scores worked out from them.
Your first name. Entered on the name screen before your results appear. If you enter a name to open the free guides, that name is kept in your browser only.
Chat messages. What you type into the “Ask Rita” chat, up to 500 characters per message.
Language and country. Your browser's language (which you can change on the site), and your country, worked out from your IP address so we can show the right currency.
Discount offers. If you spin the discount wheel or unlock the share offer, the offer and code you received.
Usage and device information. Collected automatically by our analytics tools: the screens you view, the page you came from, how you interact with the site, and technical details such as your browser and device. See Cookies & analytics.
Purchase details. Collected by our checkout provider when you buy the eBook. See Payments.
02

How information is used

We use the information above to:

Run the quiz: calculate and show your results, include your first name in your results headline and result card, and match the warning and book description to your answers.
Let you pick up where you left off on the same device.
Show prices in the right currency (USD or EUR) and send you to the matching checkout.
Run the Ask Rita chat and generate its replies.
Remember discount offers you have already received.
See how many people start and finish the quiz, which screens are reached and how the chat is used, so we can improve the site.
Sell and deliver the eBook through our checkout provider.
03

Payments

The eBook is bought on our checkout page, which uses Whop, a third-party checkout and payments platform. Prices are shown in USD by default; visitors detected in Europe (by country or site language) see EUR pricing and are sent to the EU checkout. The eBook is delivered digitally.

Payment details are entered on the checkout page and handled by the checkout provider. The quiz and sales pages themselves don't ask for payment details.

Refunds are covered by our Refund Policy.

04

AI/chat functionality

Ask Rita is an AI-powered chat. Its replies are generated automatically by an AI model, not written by a person in real time.

When you send a message, it is sent together with the earlier messages in the conversation and your selected site language to our chat service. That service passes them to an AI provider (UnoRouter) to produce a reply. Please don't share sensitive personal information, such as your full name, address or financial details, in the chat.

The conversation is kept in your browser's session storage, so it stays available as you move around the site in the same tab, and is cleared when the tab is closed. Our analytics record that the chat was opened, a message was sent, a suggestion was tapped or an error occurred — not the text of your messages.

05

Data retention

On your device
Quiz progress: no longer used after 30 days. It is replaced if you retake the quiz and removed if you clear your browser's site data.
Region check: reused for one day, then looked up again.
Discount result and free-guide name: stay on your device until you clear site data.
Chat conversation: cleared when you close the tab.
06

User rights

You control what is stored on your device: clear your browser's site data at any time, or retake the quiz to replace saved answers.

You can also contact us to ask what information we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted — for example the first name in our form responses — or to object to how it is used. If you are in Europe (including the EEA, UK and Switzerland) or another place with similar laws, you may also have the right to restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because the quiz doesn't ask for an email address or account, we may need details such as the first name you entered and roughly when, so we can find your entry. For information held by a third-party service, such as payment details at the checkout provider, you may also need to contact that service directly.

We will respond within 8–24 hours.
